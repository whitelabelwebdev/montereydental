FAQ

Do you have questions? Let’s answer some of our Most Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental visits costly?

We offer affordable dental care as we follow the latest dental fee guide. We don’t want your visits to be expensive. We believe that regular oral care such as checkups and cleanings can help keep your overall dental costs low. If you require preventative dental care we offer options to help make the fees easier to manage.

What are your hours?

We’re open Monday to Thursday from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm, Friday 8:00 am to 4:00 pm, and Saturday 8:00 am to 3:00 pm (closed on statutory long weekends). Evening and Saturday appointments make it easier to fit a visit around work or school.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we accept dental insurance and our dental team is happy to assist you in verifying your coverage (prior to treatment) as well as following up on your claim status. We also directly bill most insurance companies.

Do you welcome children?

Yes, our dental team welcomes children of all ages. We love having them around. We do our best to keep our office as child-friendly as possible.

What is your policy on rescheduling or cancelling appointments?

We always appreciate you notifying us ahead of time if you need to reschedule or cancel an appointment. We understand that emergencies or sudden changes in plans can occur. However, if it happens repeatedly without warnings, we may enforce our cancellation fee accordingly. This is to ensure that all patients get the chance to receive the treatments they need as soon as possible.
